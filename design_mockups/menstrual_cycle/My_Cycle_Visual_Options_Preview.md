## Side-by-Side Breakdown of the 4 Options

### Option 1: Interactive Period Products Explorer (`Pad`, `Clean Cloth`, `Tampon`, `Period Cup`, `Period Underwear`) + Period Blood Color Guide
![Option 1](/Users/sydneyana/.gemini/antigravity/brain/1b4d7046-cc47-4ebb-9c58-e02ba5058858/my_cycle_menstruation_opt1_products_and_colors.png)
- **Left of the Calendar:** Tap between **Pad**, **Clean Cloth**, **Tampon**, **Period Cup**, and **Period Underwear** to see a simple illustration and step-by-step instructions on how to put it on, how often to change it, and how to throw it away or wash it safely, plus clickable **First-Time Period Quick Questions** (*"Does a period hurt?"*, *"How much blood comes out?"*, *"Can I swim or play sports?"*, *"What if blood gets on my clothes?"*).
- **Below the Calendar:** 5 clickable **Period Blood Color Cards** (**Bright Red**, **Dark Red / Plum**, **Brown / Rust**, **Light Pink**, and **Gray / Bad Smell**) explaining what each color means in simple words, plus a box on **What Is Normal vs. When to Visit a Clinic**.

---

### Option 2: Interactive Body Feelings Map (*"Tap Any Spot on the Body"*) + Cramp Relief & Period Myth-Busters
![Option 2](/Users/sydneyana/.gemini/antigravity/brain/1b4d7046-cc47-4ebb-9c58-e02ba5058858/my_cycle_menstruation_opt2_body_map_and_myths.png)
- **Left of the Calendar:** An interactive body illustration with 5 clickable spots (**Mood & Head**, **Sore Breasts**, **Bloated Belly**, **Womb Cramps**, **Tired Legs / Back**). Tapping any spot explains in simple language *why* your body feels that way during your cycle and gives 3 easy ways to feel better right away.
- **Below the Calendar:** 4 **Period Facts vs. Myths** cards for first-time menstruators and all ages (*"Can I Shower & Swim?"*, *"Does a Period Mean I Can Get Pregnant?"*, *"Do Tampons or Cups Break 'Virginity'?"*, and *"What Foods Help My Body on My Period?"*).

---

### Option 3: *"How Does My Body Feel Today?"* Check-In + First-Period Bag Pouch Checklist & Blood Stain Rescue
![Option 3](/Users/sydneyana/.gemini/antigravity/brain/1b4d7046-cc47-4ebb-9c58-e02ba5058858/my_cycle_menstruation_opt3_checkin_and_period_kit.png)
- **Left of the Calendar:** Interactive pills to tap **My Bleeding / Underwear Today** (`No Blood Today`, `Light Spotting`, `Medium Flow`, `Heavy Flow`) and **My Body & Mood Today** (`Belly Cramps`, `Sore Breasts`, `Extra Tired`, `Happy & Energetic`, `Emotional / Sad`, `Hungry / Cravings`), which immediately updates the box below to reassure you why those feelings are normal for your current phase and what helps today.
- **Below the Calendar:** Two practical guides side-by-side: **What to Pack in Your Period Bag Pouch** (interactive checklist for school/work/travel) and **What If Blood Gets on My Clothes or Bed?** (why you must always rinse blood with **cold water** first, and what to do if you leak at school or outside).

### Option 4: Interactive 4-Phase Womb Storybook + *"Is My Period Normal?"* Question Cards
![Option 4](/Users/sydneyana/.gemini/antigravity/brain/1b4d7046-cc47-4ebb-9c58-e02ba5058858/my_cycle_menstruation_opt4_womb_story_and_qa.png)
- **Left of the Calendar:** 4 color-coded horizontal cards matching your calendar colors (**Menstrual Phase / Period**, **Follicular Phase**, **Ovulatory Phase**, **Luteal Phase**) with a **`YOU ARE HERE`** badge on your current phase so a first-time menstruator can see the whole month's story at a glance.
- **Below the Calendar:** 4 common first-time menstruator question cards (*"My period skipped a month! Why?"*, *"How old are girls when periods start?"*, *"Why do I see dark red jelly blobs?"*, and *"Why do I poop more on my period?"*).
